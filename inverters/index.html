02Before you specify

Five things that decide the job

Inverter mistakes
you cannot undo later.

General guidance from the supply side of the desk — worth checking before you quote.

  • 01

    Decide grid-tied or hybrid now

    A grid-tied inverter only works with the grid. A hybrid also manages a battery and can keep selected circuits live when the grid drops. Retrofitting storage to a grid-tied system later means replacing the inverter, so settle it at design time.

  • 02

    String design and the DC:AC ratio

    Module wattage, string length, site temperature, and the inverter's MPPT window all interact. A design that looks fine on a spreadsheet can clip in summer or fail to start on a cold morning.

  • 03

    Weighted efficiency and monitoring

    Peak efficiency is a marketing number; weighted efficiency is what you get across a real day. Check that the monitoring platform fits how your team works — and whether it can be handed to the system owner.

  • 04

    Battery compatibility, if it is a hybrid

    Not every hybrid inverter speaks to every battery. Confirm the pairing and the closed-loop communication before you order both, not after the pallets arrive.

  • 05

    The service path, not just the price

    Inverter failures are among the most common service calls on a solar system. Ask how a replacement is handled, how long a swap takes, and who carries the labour — before you commit to a brand.
