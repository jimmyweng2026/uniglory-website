02Before you specify

Five things that decide the job

Storage mistakes
that show up later.

General guidance from the supply side of the desk — worth checking before you quote.

  • 01

    Size against the goal, not the bill

    Storage sized to shift a specific load, ride through an outage, and shave a demand peak produces three very different numbers. Decide which job the battery has to do before anyone quotes a capacity.

  • 02

    Power (kW) is not energy (kWh)

    kW is how fast the system can deliver; kWh is how long it can keep delivering. Backup for a few critical circuits wants high power for a short window. Peak shaving wants the opposite.

  • 03

    Chemistry and safety listing

    Lithium iron phosphate is the default for stationary storage: a higher thermal runaway threshold and longer cycle life than older lithium chemistries. For commercial and project work, line up the system-level safety listing your AHJ expects before the design is fixed.

  • 04

    Siting, weight, and temperature

    Interior or exterior, clearance to walls, ventilation, ambient temperature range, and what the slab or wall can carry. Most failed storage inspections are mechanical or siting problems, not electrical ones.

  • 05

    Interconnection and incentives first

    Export limits, interconnection rules, and incentive programmes change by utility and by state — sometimes by county. Confirm the rules for the specific site before you commit to a capacity.
